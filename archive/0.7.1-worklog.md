# Worklog — 0.7.1 Self-explained deck & HUD

## Starting point

Plan 0.7 (kept in `deck-hud-plan.md`) was written after 0.6.2 shipped: the deck
explained itself through help text the player had to read, the market showed
prices but not where the profit was, the workshop quoted one delta-v number
with no curve, every dispatch handed the rocket to the autopilot, and the
flight HUD did not show cargo, vertical speed or per-stage fuel. All 14
suites were green at the start of the increment.

## Implemented

- **`js/planner.js`** — a read-only ranking layer over `market`, `parts`,
  `operations` and `flightLog`. `deltaVAt`/`payloadLimit`/`deltaVCurve` sample
  the same `rocket.evaluateBuild` the workshop runs; `legEstimate` prices one
  leg (market quote, full fuel load at the departure pad, autopilot fee or its
  absence in manual) with a predicted time that prefers the log's measured
  averages over a distance heuristic; `opportunities` and
  `contractOpportunities` rank every pad pair and open contract by profit with
  profit-per-minute as the tie-breaker; `smallestFittingType` names the type
  that unlocks a contract the selected bay cannot take. Allocations only when
  the deck calls it, never in the frame loop.
- **Dispatch deck** — a four-step live stepper (pads, rocket, pilot, launch);
  "Best trades now" ranked rows on both Dispatch and Market, each loading into
  the form on click and carrying a fit badge (`NEEDS BIGGER ROCKET` names the
  build that fits); a visual pad picker (fuel price, delivery price, appetite,
  rockets present, deliveries served) with `From`/`To` buttons writing the same
  form state as the selects; a type comparison (bay, delta-v at the payload,
  predicted profit) with `OVER DEMAND` marks; a capacity-vs-demand bar that
  says "slide to X for $Y more" or "bigger rocket unlocks Z"; a `PROFIT / MIN`
  summary row.
- **Launch modes** — a segmented `Autopilot | Manual` control plus two
  launch buttons that emphasise the selected mode. The manual path runs the
  same `operations.evaluateLeg` gate, skips the profile lock, disables the
  profile select and pays no autopilot fee; `mission.beginLeg` starts a manual
  leg cold (throttle zero, nose up, guidance off) and a return leg of a manual
  order stays manual.
- **`js/builder.js`** — a delta-v vs payload canvas plot in the workshop,
  sampled through `planner.deltaVCurve`, design payload marked, bay limit as a
  vertical line, caption quoting delta-v at 0 kg, at the design payload, at the
  limit and the launch TWR. Redrawn on workshop input only.
- **Flight HUD (`js/render.js`)** — a `PILOT & CARGO` panel: the pilot mode
  row (`AUTOPILOT · Balanced` / `MANUAL · YOU FLY`), vertical speed with an
  arrow coloured against the landing limit, cargo with `FRAGILE` and the
  payout at risk, remaining ideal staged delta-v with a bar against what it
  launched with, total fuel, one row per stage (burning / next / spent, fuel
  bars, `NEXT · SPACE` vs `NEXT · AUTO`), and the control rows `AIM`,
  `THRUST`, `STAGE`, `PILOT` naming the keys for the current mode. The map
  draws the aim tick, the prograde velocity vector and a target bearing.
- **Manual launch & handoff clarity** — while a manual rocket sits at pad
  level with no throttle, the map draws a callout on the rocket
  (`HOLD SHIFT TO LIFT OFF`, with the aim/stage/handoff keys under it) that
  disappears on thrust; the flight strip names the pilot mode and carries the
  `Take over (A)` / `Hand back to autopilot (A)` button (amber, beside
  `Open deck`), which is the same toggle the `A` key presses.
- **Routes** — every route card quotes predicted profit per leg and per
  minute for its next leg alongside the measured averages from the log,
  labelled `PREDICTED` and `MEASURED`; the route-review dialog re-quotes as
  its pads, payload or type change.

## Validation

- **`experiments/planner-test.js`** (new) checks the planner against the real
  rules: the curve and every sample match `evaluateBuild` bit for bit;
  `legEstimate` matches `market.quote` with its fee, fuel, profit, margin and
  rate arithmetic; manual drops the fee; opportunities cover every ordered pad
  pair, carry `min(bay, demand)` and are ranked by profit; open contracts rank
  fits-first, an over-bay contract keeps its row and names the smallest
  fitting type, `smallestFittingType` returns null past every bay; the time
  estimate is the calibrated heuristic on an empty log and the measured
  average across both directions once the log has legs; `measuredRoute`
  averages cash and time per route.
- **`experiments/render-smoke-test.js`** extended, against the real
  `index.html`: the stepper states, ranked rows with profit per minute, pad
  cards and their `To` button, the type comparison plus workshop row, the
  capacity bar, the workshop caption (design, bay limit, launch TWR, follows
  the payload slider), and the manual path end to end — the manual button
  starts the leg cold with `usedAutopilot` false and no guidance spend on the
  ledger, the strip names `MANUAL · YOU FLY` with its handoff button, the HUD
  draws the callout and the pilot/cargo rows, one click on the strip button
  hands the rocket to `AUTOPILOT · Balanced` and flips the button and HUD, a
  second click hands it back, and the adjudicated delivery pays the full
  quote with no autopilot fee line.
- Mutation checks, each restored afterwards: making `beginLeg` ignore the
  manual flag, charging the fee on every delivery, and reversing the
  planner's profit sort were each caught by an assertion.
- All 15 suites pass. Regressions byte-identical to their logs:
  `competitor-sweep.js 20 20261006`, `balance.js 20261004 4 2` (the recorded
  run is `logs/0.5.2-balance-pressure.txt`, the ordinary economy since 0.5.2),
  `balance.js 20261004 4 2 damage`
  (`logs/0.5.2-balance-pressure-damage.txt`), `campaign-sweep.js 40 verdant`,
  `campaign-sweep.js 60 campaign`.
- Nothing was added to the frame loop or the physics step: the panels are
  rebuilt on deck renders and revisions, the plot on workshop input, and the
  HUD strings it needs cached are rebuilt only when the pilot or profile
  changes.

## Not verified

- The new markup, CSS and canvas panels have only been exercised through the
  fake DOM and a stub 2D context. Nobody has looked at the pad picker, the
  opportunity rows, the workshop plot or the cold-start callout in a browser,
  so layout and styling are unchecked — in particular the callout's place
  above the rocket at each world's camera scale.
- The manual leg in the smoke test is adjudicated at the pad rather than
  hand-flown; a real mouse-and-throttle flight has not been played.

## Known weak spots

- `flight.usedAutopilot` follows the last handoff: a pilot who takes over
  just before touchdown pays no fee even if the autopilot flew most of the
  leg (the 0.3.5 rule that the fee is for the guidance). The reverse —
  handing back at the last moment — pays it.
- The planner's cost model is a full fuel load with no turnaround, overhaul
  or repair, so its profit ranks can overstate a worn rocket's take; the
  dispatch card's evaluated cost stays the number that is charged.
- The time heuristic (20–120 s from distance) is calibrated to the shipped
  envelope at 1x only; a player at 4x sees predicted minutes pass faster
  than wall-clock minutes.

## Next step

pad picker layout, the plot's readability at real DPI, and the callout over
the rocket. Then make the ranked list act on what it reveals: a "build the
rocket that fits" button on a `NEEDS BIGGER ROCKET` row that opens the

# Plan 0.6 — Milestones & Campaign Arc

Subject scope: a company-progression layer on top of the existing world,
autopilot, market, damage and competitor systems. The 0.5 sandbox already flies,
fails, repairs, competes and settles on a real deterministic budget; 0.6 gives
that sandbox named goals, visible company standing, a first "win" condition and
a clean handoff between worlds. It introduces no new physics, no second flight
model, no per-frame work.

This plan ships in two increments:

- **0.6.1 Company milestones** — named delivery/cash/reputation goals, a
  milestone ticker in the deck, milestone rewards, and a first end-of-arc
  settlement against Skybolt Logistics on the starting world.
- **0.6.2 Inter-world campaign** — milestone-gated world unlocks, a company
  record that carries reputation (but not fleet or cash) across worlds, and a
  final "dominant courier" condition across the whole planet set.

## 0.6.1 — Company milestones

### Milestone ladder

A milestone is a named threshold on the starting world (Verdant). Each tracks
one measurable — total deliveries, delivered kilograms, cash earned net of
startup capital, reputation, rival-beat margin, or surviving-fragile-cargo
count. Milestones are strictly ordered: only the current milestone is active,
and it is checked every time the player settles a leg on the deck. Hitting it
pays a one-time cash bonus (or unlocks a build feature, see below), marks it
complete, and activates the next one.

The ladder is tuned so a careful reference-build player hits each milestone on
a real, profitable route chain, not by grinding standing service on the near
hop. Failure does not block progress except through the cash and reputation it
costs; a milestone that asks for "10 successful contracts" simply does not tick
when a leg crashes.

Proposed Verdant ladder (subject to tuning):

1. **First receipt** — deliver 1 standing-service leg to Eastport. Reward:
   $2,000 small-business grant, to recover from a first bad hop.
2. **On the board** — complete 3 contracts from the Verdant board. Reward:
   unlocks the *Gentle* profile for the player (it exists but is shown as
   locked until then).
3. **Haulier** — deliver 400 kg of cumulative surviving cargo across all pads.
   Reward: $5,000 regional shipping grant.
4. **Reliable service** — reach reputation 65 without a crash in the previous
   5 legs. Reward: $8,000 and marks the rival's skill growth to accelerate
   slightly (Skybolt notices the player).
5. **Long reach** — complete one contract to Farport (the far pad). Reward:
   $10,000 inter-pad licence, unlocks the world-switch menu for Tinmoon.
6. **Market corner** — hold a higher cash total than Skybolt and have served at
   least as many deliveries to every pad on the planet. Reward: declares the
   player "Dominant courier – Verdant" and is the 0.6.1 arc settlement.

The rival does *not* get its own milestone ladder; it is the foil whose
progress the player measures against. Its reputation and skill grow as in
0.5.2 and are reported in the standings, unchanged.

### Milestone state and rewards

Milestone state lives in a new `R.campaign` module. It is seeded by the world
seed like the market, reset per world in `operations.initialize`, and recorded
on the per-world save shape that main.restart already replaces. A completed
milestone shows its reward amount and a check; the active milestone shows its
current numerator / denominator. Progress never moves while physics is running;
it is updated only from the existing `applyLegResult` finish path on the deck,
after the leg result and any rival turn settle, so it cannot run mid-flight.

The *Gentle profile unlock* is the first non-cash reward. It is currently
selectable from every dispatch card; after 0.6.1 it shows as LOCKED with the
unlocking milestone named, and the dispatch card refuses it until the milestone
clears. Routes remember the profile they were saved with and refuse to auto-launch
on a locked profile (showing the lock reason in place of READY).

### Deck presentation

The **Market** tab grows a small milestone strip above the scoreboard:

```
MILESTONE 3 / 6  ·  Haulier          280 / 400 kg delivered
[ ][ ][ ][ ][ ][ ]
```

Clicking the strip expands a panel showing the ladder, rewards, and for each
future milestone the thresholds still hidden behind it. The Market tab's
scoreboard already shows player and rival side by side; the final milestone's
"dominant courier" settlement compares those numbers verbally
("Ahead by $X and N deliveries" or "Skybolt still leads by $Y").

A campaign-toast message fires once per milestone completion (deck.announce),
consistent with existing route-enabled / paused toasts. Victory (milestone 6)
shows a longer settlement card naming the planet, the cash lead, the delivery
count and reputation at finish; dismissing it returns the player to the deck
and leaves them free to keep flying on the world. There is no forced reset.

### Milestone validation

- Milestone progress is deterministic with the same seed: a seeded 20-turn run
  advances the ladder the same distance twice.
- A failed flight (crashed or cargo lost) does not grant delivery progress,
  does grant cash-loss "progress backwards" for cash milestones, and cannot
  award a milestone mid-flight.
- The cash milestone uses net cash *above the starting* $40,000, so a player who
  loses money does not regress past zero progress.
- `node experiments/campaign-test.js` checks deterministic progress, reward
  payouts, the Gentle-profile lock, Farport unlock gating, and that a crash
  does not award a milestone.
- The milestone ladder is reproduced byte-for-byte against a saved log
  `experiments/logs/0.6.1-milestones.txt` from a seeded 40-turn run on Verdant.
- All 13 pre-existing test suites still pass.

## 0.6.2 — Inter-world campaign

### Reputation carry and unlocks

Once milestone 5 clears on Verdant, the world menu allows travel to Tinmoon. A
world switch keeps the player's *reputation* baseline (clamped to 60, so a new
world starts more trustworthy than the 50 default) and awards the rival a
symmetric reputation carry for that world. Fleet, cash, routes and rocket
types are rebuilt per world — the existing `main.restart` already resets them,
and 0.6.2 only seeds reputation and campaign progress from the shared carry.

Cash does not transfer: each world has its own starter capital, so progression
is through skill and reputation rather than a snowballing bank account.
Unlocks — the *Gentle* profile, the world list, the milestone display — travel
with the campaign.

Each world has its own six-milestone ladder tuned to its own distances, prices
and flight envelope. The final milestone on each world is "Dominant courier –
<World>", requiring the same cash-and-delivery lead over Skybolt.

### Pan-planet victory

A separate global campaign tally tracks "dominant courier" settlements per
world. When a player is dominant on all four worlds (Verdant, Tinmoon, Cinder,
Gossamer) the game shows a final arc-complete toast and credits, then returns
to free play. The rival never loses its budget or fleet between worlds; each
world's Skybolt instance is independent but starts with slightly higher skill
when the player carries higher reputation (the rival has heard of you).

The world-picker menu shows each world's milestone count and dominant-courier
state (locked / in progress / dominant) so the player can see where they still
have work.

### Campaign validation

- Reputation carry is deterministic: switching worlds from the same save seed
  seeds the rival with the same skill offset.
- A player who never unlocks Tinmoon cannot reach it through the world menu;
  the milestone gate is enforced in `menu.selectWorld` as well as in the UI.
- Dominant-courier state persists across a world switch and survives a browser
  reload via the existing run-reset path.
- `node experiments/campaign-test.js` exercises the world-switch path and
  asserts reputation carry, cash reset, fleet reset and rival skill offset.
- A seeded run across all four worlds records `experiments/logs/0.6.2-campaign.txt`,
  reproducible byte-for-byte, showing milestones hit per world and the final
  dominant-courier tally.
- Balance and competitor sweeps remain in their existing bands; the campaign
  layer is passive reporting and gating, never runs inside the physics step,
  and does not alter existing flight economics.

## Acceptance criteria for 0.6

- A fresh run starts with milestone 1 active and the Gentle profile locked; a
  reference Verdant player reaches milestone 6 in a measured and repeatable
  number of legs without needing to grind.
- The milestone strip updates only on the deck, never during flight, and never
  awards a milestone for a failed leg.
- Skybolt is the foil for milestone 6 but its existing 0.5.2 behaviour is
  unchanged on every other axis — bidding, flights, cash, pressure, reputation.
- World unlocks are gated by the milestone that names them; the world menu
  shows lock reasons and unlock progress.
- All 0.5 acceptance criteria still hold; all earlier tests remain green; no
  allocations enter the frame loop or physics step.

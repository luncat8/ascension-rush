# Worklog — 0.6.2 Inter-world campaign (and the rest of 0.6.1)

## Starting point

0.6.1 had shipped the Verdant ladder, its strip in the Market tab and the
Gentle lock. Its own worklog listed what was missing: no measured run of the
ladder, no settlement card for milestone 6, a test that covered only the first
milestone. 0.6.2 was not started. All 14 suites and the three 0.5.2 regression
logs were green at that point.

## Review of what 0.6.1 left

- Campaign state was one object rebuilt per world, with a `carry` argument
  threaded through `operations.initialize`. Nothing outlived a world switch
  except what the caller remembered to pass, and nothing survived a reload.
- Milestones measured company totals only, so one leg could clear several
  "tasks" it had never been asked to do.
- The ladder's targets had never been flown. Measured on totals, one Tinmoon
  leg (leg 5) cleared milestones 2–5 together.

## Implemented

- **`js/campaign.js`** is split into a **record** (reputation, unlocks, cleared
  milestones, one settlement per world) and a per-world **state** (ladder,
  active rung, counters). The record is saved to `localStorage` on every settled
  leg once the page has called `campaign.restore()`; node scripts never call it,
  so tests and sweeps stay storage-free. `campaign.reset()` starts over.
- **Eight measures** (`deliveries`, `contracts`, `contractTo`, `fragile`,
  `cargoKg`, `netCash`, `reputationClean`, `dominant`) in one table of
  functions. `contracts`, `contractTo` and `fragile` count from the moment the
  milestone activates (`state.taskBase`); the rest are company totals.
- **Dominant courier** needs more cash than the rival and every pad served at
  least as often as the rival and at least `dominantMinPerPad` (3) times.
  With a minimum of 1 the settlement fell out of the route chain by accident.
- **Four ladders** in `constants.campaign.worlds`, six milestones each, grants
  in the world's own money. Verdant opens Tinmoon, Tinmoon opens Cinder, Cinder
  opens Gossamer.
- **World switch**: `operations.initialize()` takes no carry any more. It reads
  the start reputation from `campaign.initialize()` (record reputation clamped
  to 50–60) and hands `campaign.rivalCarry()` to `competitor.initialize`: the
  same reputation head start, 0.005 skill per carried point, and `noticed` when
  the world's notice milestone was already cleared.
- **Profile lock** is `operations.profileLock(profileId)`, used by dispatch,
  route readiness and the deck. It names the milestone and world that open the
  profile and does not apply to a rival dispatch.
- **Deck**: the strip moved under the tabs so it shows on every tab, and
  expands into the full ladder. A cleared milestone is listed under the debrief
  with its reward and the next goal. The dominant milestone opens a settlement
  card at the deck (never over another dialog), with a button to the world menu.
- **World menu** (`js/menu.js`): `menu.selectWorld` refuses a locked world;
  cards show locked-with-reason, `n / 6 MILESTONES` or dominant with the
  margin; "Start a new campaign" erases the record behind a second click.
- **`experiments/campaign-sweep.js`**: a reference player through the real
  game with the damage model on. It keeps the granted rocket, flies one-way
  legs, prefers cargo the active milestone asks for, then profit, and
  repositions to a pad whose board holds a qualifying contract.
- Cleanups: `util.money` shared by the deck and the campaign; the reputation
  default in `competitor.js` reads `constants.campaign.startReputation`;
  `constants.autopilot` indentation; the throwaway `experiments/tmp-*.js`
  probes are gone.

## Tuning

Targets were moved against the sweep until the reference player's milestones
were spread over a session instead of bunched on a few legs:

| change | from | to | why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Verdant Haulier | 400 kg | 1,000 kg | the ladder was bunched at the original targets |
| `dominantMinPerPad` | 1 | 3 | "served at least once" was met by the route chain itself |
| Tinmoon Ore train | | 1,000 kg | retuned with the task-from-activation change |
| Tinmoon In the black | | $5,000 net | legs there net roughly $250–$1,100 |
| Gossamer Long haul | | 1 contract | a second contract to one pad stalled the run |
| Cinder Reliable service | REP 70 | REP 75 | cleared on leg 13 together with Careful hands |
| Gossamer Heavy air | 800 kg | 1,200 kg | cleared on leg 7, two legs after Sky trade |

Grants were sized against each world's leg income and not tuned further.

## Not verified

- The new markup and CSS were exercised through the fake DOM only. Nobody has
  looked at the strip, the ladder, the settlement card or the locked world
  cards in a browser, so layout and styling are unchecked.
- The reference player never fails a flight in either log. The leg counts are
  a clean-play pace, not what a player who crashes will see.

## Known weak spots

- On Cinder and Gossamer the dominant settlement still lands on the same leg
  as milestone 5: by then the reference player already leads on cash and has
  served every pad three times.
- Skybolt is no contest on Gossamer (3 deliveries in 16 turns) and trails by
  $80,000 on Cinder. The carry gives it +0.05 skill, which does not change
  that.
- Cinder's first leg nets about $24,000, so its ladder's grants are small next
  to the trade and its cash race is decided early.

## Next step

Look at the deck in a browser first and fix whatever the fake DOM could not
show. Then make the later worlds a contest: measure Skybolt per world with
`competitor-sweep.js` on Tinmoon, Cinder and Gossamer, and give it a per-world
starting skill or courier that puts its deliveries near the player's, so that
"dominant" on world four is harder than on world one. A failure-prone
reference player in `campaign-sweep.js` would show how the ladders read after
a crash or two.

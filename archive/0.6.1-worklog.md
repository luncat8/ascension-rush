# Worklog — 0.6.1 Company milestones

## Starting point

0.5.2's competitor, shared-board pricing and scoreboard shipped and were
reproduced byte-for-byte against `experiments/logs/0.5.2-competitor.txt`,
`experiments/logs/0.5.2-balance-pressure.txt` and
`experiments/logs/0.5.2-balance-pressure-damage.txt`. The review found one
small fix worth making before 0.6 work: `competitor.fly()` did not advance
`simTime`, so if any future effect read simTime during a rival flight it would
see zero. The loop now advances `simTime` by the fixed step before each
control/physics update; all existing sweeps still reproduce exactly.

## Plan review

The 0.5 plan closed out with a fully tuned rival. The next arc needs a goal,
not a new physics system: the existing game flies, fails, repairs, competes
and settles deterministically, but the player has no named objective other than
"keep making money." The `0.6-plan.md` drafted here covers company milestones
(0.6.1) and an inter-world campaign (0.6.2). 0.6.1 is deliberately small: a
six-step ladder on Verdant, cash and unlock rewards, a milestone strip in the
Market tab, and a first dominant-courier settlement that uses the existing
scoreboard numbers. No new flight model, no per-frame work.

## Implemented

- **`js/campaign.js`** owns the milestone ladder, progress counters (deliveries,
  contracts delivered, surviving kg, clean-streak reputation, per-destination
  contract deliveries and the dominant-courier comparison), per-world unlocks
  and reputation-carry state. It is seeded per world like the market;
  `campaign.onLegSettled(result)` is invoked from `mission.finish` on the deck
  after `applyLegResult` and the rival turn complete, so progress only moves
  when the player reaches the deck. A milestone that fires returns its
  summary to the debrief record and triggers a deck announcement.
- **`js/constants.js`** adds `constants.campaign` with the Verdant ladder,
  unlock identifiers and a `noticedSkillGrowth` step that increases Skybolt's
  skill once the player reaches "Reliable service". The competitor constants
  gain `noticeReputation` and `noticedSkillGrowth`; the rest of the existing
  competitor tuning is unchanged.
- **`js/operations.js`** accepts a `carry` argument in `initialize()` (used by
  world switches), seeds player reputation from the carry, calls
  `campaign.initialize(carry)` before building the reference fleet, and
  passes the carry into `competitor.initialize`. A new
  `LOCKED_PROFILE` refusal prevents dispatching a profile the campaign has
  not unlocked.
- **`js/competitor.js`** accepts `carry` in `initialize()` and applies the
  rival skill offset; all other starting values are unchanged. The fly loop
  adds `game.simTime += step`.
- **`js/mission.js`** calls `R.campaign.onLegSettled` after the rival turn,
  attaches the milestone award to `game.lastReport`, and fires a deck
  announcement so the player sees it on touchdown.
- **`js/deck.js`** renders a gold-tinted milestone strip above the scoreboard:
  milestone number, title, current/target progress and a six-dot indicator
  (done / active / future). `fillProfiles` dispatches locked profile options
  with "(locked)" labels and falls back to `balanced` when the saved profile
  is locked.
- **`index.html` / `css/style.css`** add the milestone strip markup and its
  gold stylesheet. `campaign.js` is loaded after `competitor.js`.

# Worklog — 0.3.10 coast follow-up

## Changes

- The engine-off trace was capped at 256 points, but a 120-second forecast
  sampled every 42 fixed steps needs up to 343 samples plus its endpoints. A
  long valid forecast could therefore display an impact marker without drawing
  the end of its path. Derive trace capacity from the forecast horizon and
  stride, including space for both endpoints. A 108.5-second Tinmoon coast now
  tests the final ground point; an out-of-horizon coast tests the partial trace.
- Correct the README: hold speed is not an escape-speed cap. An out-of-horizon
  coast need not be unbound, and even a rocket above escape energy can hit the
  surface on its incoming trajectory.
- Sample a real Tinmoon reference delivery at 120 fps and 2x. It delivers and
  passes through a bound coast beyond the horizon, an unbound coast beyond the
  horizon, and an unbound coast that hits the ground. Render smoke tests check
  that the coast row, marker and separate `UNBOUND` indication reflect those
  distinct states. Existing tests also check the forecast against a real
  engine-off Verdant flight and sample all atmospheric-world flights.

## Balance decision

Keep Gossamer's fuel price and the +10–20% target unchanged for now. The
24-leg damage-on steady-service sample is only one price path per seed, not a
statistical guarantee: seeds 20261004–20261007 produce Gossamer margins of
+9.3%, +10.7%, +8.8%, and +6.8%; Verdant also misses on the last seed (+8.3%).
A price change based on one narrow miss, or widening a target to hide it, would
not address that variation. The command was `node experiments/balance.js SEED
4 2 damage`; the steady-state section always uses 24 legs per world.

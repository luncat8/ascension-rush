# 0.4.4 Balance pass · node experiments/balance.js 20261004 4 2
# A seeded Monte Carlo through the real scheduler, autopilot and physics: every
# bucket flies `operations.dispatch` for `legs` legs per repetition and records
# what the flight log says the leg booked. One bucket is one world × one
# distance bracket × one stage count × one payload rung of the planet's ladder.
#
# The margin of a delivered leg is `cashDelta / reward`: the payout less the 10%
# autopilot fee, the fuel it refuelled, the structure the turnaround replaced
# and the overhaul it bought. The granted, fully fuelled rocket a chain opens on
# buys no fuel, so its first leg is counted apart (`grant`) and left out of the
# histogram: it is the run's bonus, not the service's steady state.
#
# Prices in force: verdant fuel 4.1 / steel 2.5 / delivery 18 · tinmoon 2.6 / 2.0
# / 3.5 · cinder 5.5 / 3.6 / 130 · gossamer 5.0 / 2.2 / 20. Market: urgencyBonus
# 1.0, fragileBonus 0.5, distanceBonus 1.7, padSpread 0.18, driftPerTurn 0.03.
# startingCash 40000.

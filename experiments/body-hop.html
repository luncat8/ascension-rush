CHART LAB / ONE LOG-POLAR MAP

The probe is the camera's target, and once it is above the air the chart is read from its own place: the log pole travels out of the world and stops on the surface under the probe, so the hull holds the origin line, the world curves away below it, and every other body is a lens charted from that pole. Drag the probe anywhere to fly it; fly it onto a body and the chart unwraps that body into flat ground. Drag the sky down to unwrap the chart onto whatever you grabbed, drag sideways to look around it, wheel to zoom, click a body to seat the chart on it.

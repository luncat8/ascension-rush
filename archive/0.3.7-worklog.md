# Worklog 0.3.7 — Inter-planet sky, escape speed & adaptive projection

## Plan

  plan): **0.3.7** for the visual sky slice and **0.3.8** as a headless
  Lagrange-point / multi-body-gravity experiment that stays out of the flight

  `AGENTS.md` and a note to the README's plan index.

## Implementation

- `js/constants.js`: `constants.view` gains the world-switch morph seconds,
  sky-band fraction and body-radius clamps; `constants.sky` holds the shared
  star / planet / moon catalog (distances and radii in active-planet radii),
  atmosphere density fraction, approach span, easing time, trail and trace
  capacities.
- `js/physics.js`: `gravitationalParameter`, `escapeSpeedAtAltitude`,
  `circularSpeedAtAltitude`, `atmosphereTop` and `isUnbound` derived from the
  same `mu = g0*R^2` whose derivative is `gravityAtAltitude`. No change to
  `physics.step`.
- `js/camera.js` / `js/coords.js`: the camera now owns `logScale` and
  `ceiling`; `setRange` is the only writer and `coords` reads them for both
  the forward and inverse transform. A planet change resets to that world's
  defaults, a resize keeps the eased range.
- `js/sky.js`: catalog focus by log radial distance, an eased target for the
  log knee and ceiling, a smoothstep morph of gravity / radius / air ceiling
  on a world switch, and an azimuthal equidistant body projection with
  analytic angular radius, horizon hiding and star-lit shading.
- `js/trajectory.js`: `rollout` optionally writes a strided polyline into the
  caller-owned `trajectory.trace`, so the coast path costs no second
  integration. `update` fills it; the old four-argument calls are unchanged.
- `js/flight-path.js`: fixed-capacity ring of flown positions, reset on a new
  flight record or a world switch, sampled on the simulation clock.
- `js/render.js`: space shade, air band with a cached ceiling label, celestial
  discs, adaptive decade altitude marks, coast trace and two-tone flown trail,
  plus `ESCAPE` / `UNBOUND` readouts in the flight HUD and an `ESCAPE SPEED`
  row on the deck's world panel.
- `js/main.js`: `sky.update` on real frame time and `flightPath.sample` on
  simulation time each frame; `restart` begins the morph before the immediate
  redraw. `index.html` loads the two new classic scripts.

## Validation

- `node experiments/sky-test.js` (new): GM / escape / circular speed
  identities, atmosphere ceiling on airless and atmospheric worlds, finite and
  invertible round trips at an eased log scale and ceiling, monotonic screen
  y, world-switch morph endpoints, body angular radius and horizon hiding,
  coast trace endpoints, and trail ring resets.
- Full regression set passes: `coords`, `aerodynamics`, `flight`, `builder`,
  `parts`, `service`, `render-smoke`, `autopilot`, `trajectory`, `operations`,
  `market`, `damage`, `competitor`, `campaign`, `planner`, `sky`.
- `node experiments/envelope.js verdant 0` still reports 12/12 delivered with
  the same time / altitude / peak-Q / touchdown figures;
  `node experiments/impact-accuracy.js 6 3` reports 0 states over tolerance;
  `node experiments/build-sweep.js 60 1` reports 0 regressions against the
  dry baseline. No physics input changed.
- `render-smoke-test.js` now loads the sky modules and stubs `clip`; it asserts
  the star, a moon, the atmosphere ceiling and surface escape speed are drawn.

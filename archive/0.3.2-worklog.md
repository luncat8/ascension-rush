# Worklog — 0.3.2 Flight-envelope autopilot

## Plan review
- Measured before tuning. The acceptance criteria ask for every reference route
  delivered on all four worlds, but the starting point was Tinmoon 10/12, and a
  frame-loop run (one control update per frame, as `main.js` does) showed a
  *different* pair of Tinmoon routes crashing at different frame rates and time
  scales. The cause was a pre-existing guidance defect, fixed first (see
  below); the three other worlds were already 12/12 at every setting.
- The angle-of-attack limit as written (clamp the axis to the nearer end of the
  airflow axis) does not survive contact with the hop trajectory. The Q fade
  removes forward thrust and keeps lift, so the thrust axis tilts toward
  vertical and AoA *rises* as Q nears the cap; a clamp then forbids exactly the
  sideways thrust that holds the rocket up (peak q·sin α went from 19.9 to
  24 kPa) and the nearer-axis tie flips the nose through broadside. It ships as
  a backstop: eased in across the band, sticky about the end of the axis it is
  on, and never tighter than the angle at which the budget can still lift the
  rocket. The plan's 0.3.2 section now states these rules.
- A plain acceleration cap also needed a floor (1.7 × surface gravity) and
  ignition needed to honour it; both are described under Implemented.

## Implemented
- **Guidance fix.** The horizontal position term was `|dx| * gain + 2`, so the
  speed target never reached zero and flipped sign over the pad. On Tinmoon
  (vertical channel in free fall) the rocket hunted ±2.5 m/s with the heading
  swinging through straight down under thrust; removing the offset makes all 12
  Tinmoon routes land at every frame rate / time scale (worst touchdown sink
  rate 3.8 of 8 m/s, pad error 0 m). No world's hop or long-route time moved by
  more than 1.3 s (Cinder's short hop +1.2 s, Tinmoon's long hop +1.3 s).
- **Limits** (`js/constants.js`, `js/planets.js`, `js/autopilot.js`). Four
  planet-tunable keys in the existing `planet.flight` path: `maxDynamicPressure`
  (Pa), `qFadeStartFraction` (0.75), `maxThrustAcceleration` (m/s²),
  `maxAngleOfAttackDeg`. A null, absent or non-positive limit is off. They are
  resolved once per flight (cache keyed on planet and profile, dropped by
  `autopilot.reset()`), with degrees converted to radians there.
- **Command shaping.** The acceleration cap lowers the guidance budget, so the
  climb, brake and descent profiles plan with the thrust they may use, and is
  floored at `minThrustToWeight` (1.7) × surface gravity. One flow-aligned step
  (`commandThrust`) then fades the positive along-flow part of the request
  linearly across the Q band, applies the AoA backstop, and converts to heading
  and throttle (throttle is the request's projection on the permitted axis).
  Q is computed from the live state with the shared `aerodynamics.dynamicPressure`
  helper, not last step's telemetry. No allocation in the update loop.
- **Ignition.** `rocket.separateStage(state, ignitionThrottle)` (default 1, so a
  player's staging is unchanged); autopilot staging passes the lever's current
  position and an autopilot launch lights the engine at the throttle the
  guidance wants. Without this a capped rocket spiked at every light-off.
- **Profiles.** `constants.autopilotProfiles`: **Balanced** (the planet's limits)
  and **Gentle** (named multipliers: Q × 0.7, thrust acceleration × 0.8, AoA ×
  0.7, then the floors). The builder has a profile select under the autopilot
  toggle; the choice is snapshotted into `flight.autopilotProfile` at launch and
  the debrief's autopilot-fee line names it.
- **HUD.** A `LIMITER` row under `AUTOPILOT` shows the active limits (`Q LIMIT`,
  `ACCEL LIMIT`, `AOA LIMIT` or combinations, from a preallocated table indexed
  by flag bits) and the flight's profile; manual flight shows `OFF` with the
  same Q / AoA / peak-Q telemetry.
- **Measurement.** `flight.peakAppliedThrustAcceleration` (thrust ÷ start-of-step
  mass, the same mass as the available figure and the guidance) joins the
  report; the debrief now quotes it.
- **Experiments.** `experiments/harness.js` (shared flight loop in the frame
  loop's order, limit overrides, flight-quality metrics), `envelope.js`
  (profile or `off`, fps and time scale, applied acceleration, limiter seconds,
  re-climb and axis flips), `autopilot-test.js` (new suite),
  `build-sweep.js` (random builds, limits off vs profiles), and recorded output
  in `experiments/logs/0.3.2-*.txt`.

## Limits chosen
Starting points came from the unconstrained reference peaks (Q cap about 0.9 ×
the peak, acceleration cap about the reference build's own applied peak) and
were refined against the route runs. Gentle's effective values include the floors.

| World | Q cap (fade from 75%) | Accel cap | AoA backstop | Gentle: Q / accel / AoA |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Verdant | 28 kPa | 22 m/s² | 55° | 19.6 kPa / 17.6 m/s² / 38.5° |
| Tinmoon | none | 16 m/s² | none | — / 12.8 m/s² / — |
| Cinder | 70 kPa | 34 m/s² | 60° | 49 kPa / 28.05 m/s² (gravity floor) / 42° |
| Gossamer | 60 kPa | 22 m/s² | 35° | 42 kPa / 17.6 m/s² / 24.5° |

The weight-support floor raises Gentle's effective AoA limit to about 58° on
Verdant, 61° on Cinder and 39° on Gossamer, marginally *looser* than Balanced's
55°, 60° and 35°: a lower thrust cap needs a wider angle to hold altitude, so the
plan's "tighter angle-of-attack envelope" for Gentle cannot be delivered in
sideways flight. Gentle's measurable gains are in Q and acceleration.

## Observed, not changed
- A build whose middle stage has `T / m` below local gravity (e.g. 230 kg of fuel
  between a 1,000 kg booster and a 1,470 kg upper stage on Gossamer) cannot hover
  after staging, and dry-stage-only staging never brings the strong upper stage in
  in time. About 6% of random builds fail with no limits (5% with Balanced, 4%
  with Gentle); the limits change when it happens, which is the source of the few
  regressions. A staging
  rule that lights a stronger stage when the active one cannot hover would fix the
  class.
- While coasting down the brake profile the horizontal request hovers around zero
  and its sign dithers (heading command ±90° at about 0.00 throttle). It is in the
  unlimited baseline, harmless to the flight, and is why axis-side flips are a poor
  oscillation measure.
- `flight.usedAutopilot` is fixed at launch, so toggling the autopilot mid-flight
  neither charges nor refunds its fee. That belongs with the 0.4 economy work.
- Tinmoon's 14 m small rebound before touchdown predates this slice.

## Next step
Implement 0.3.3: the bounded, drag-aware coast-impact predictor on the shared
gravity and aerodynamic helpers (validate it against high-resolution engine-off
rollouts with the harness, including wrap-around, vacuum and zero speed) and the
debrief with measured touchdown speeds and target-pad error. Keep it out of the
guidance loop. Consider a small staging improvement afterwards (stage early when
the active stage cannot hover) since the build sweep shows it is the main remaining
source of autopilot failures.

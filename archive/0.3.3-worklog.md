# Worklog — 0.3.3 Drag-aware coast-impact prediction and debrief

## Plan review
- The plan filed the predictor under `autopilot.js`. It ships as `js/trajectory.js`
  instead: a coast forecast is a property of the flight state, not of the
  autopilot (the 0.3.2 marker was autopilot-only, so the pilot who needs it most
  — a manual one timing a landing burn — never saw it), the autopilot stays a
  command layer, and `main.js` drives the forecast for both.
- The plan asked for "a coarse fixed prediction step" without naming one, so the
  step was measured before being chosen (`experiments/impact-accuracy.js`,
  `experiments/logs/0.3.3-impact-accuracy.txt`, both variants recorded). At 1/30 s
  the forecast missed tolerance on 8 of 1,176 states — worst 150.6 m, 1.84% of
  predicted range, all of them dense air at speed (Cinder and Gossamer). At the
  live fixed step (1/120 s) every state is inside tolerance: worst 37.8 m
  (0.45% of range) on the synthetic edge grid and 1.6–2.3 m on the states the
  marker is actually shown for, for about 0.1 ms per forecast (up to 0.5 ms on a
  near-horizon coast). The live step also makes a rollout bit-identical to an
  engine-off `physics.step` flight, which `trajectory-test.js` asserts to 1e-9 m
  and 1e-9 s; a second, coarser model could only ever be compared approximately.
  The plan's 0.3.3 section now states the choice and the reason.
- Three display rules were missing from the plan and are now in it: the marker is
  drawn from the *periodic copy* of the impact longitude (a long westward coast
  otherwise leaves the screen at ground the player can see), the forecast is
  shown in manual flight, and its colour separates a delivery on the target pad
  from a safe landing elsewhere and from an arrival outside the world's
  touchdown limits — the plan's borrowed MechJeb idea of distinguishing a
  projected impact from a landing.
- Added a proposed **0.3.4 — Staging authority** section so the failure class the
  0.3.2 build sweep found (a middle stage that cannot hover, never staged early)
  survives in the plan rather than only in a worklog.

## Implemented
- **`js/trajectory.js`.** `rollout(state, dt, maxSteps, out)` integrates
  engine-off flight in the live step's order: gravity and drag sampled at the
  start of the step, semi-implicit Euler, the same 200 m/s² net-acceleration
  clip, the same linear ground-crossing interpolation. Heading and mass are held
  — a coasting rocket burns nothing and this model has no attitude dynamics.
  `predictCoast` runs it at `constants.rocket.fixedStep` under a 120 s horizon;
  `update(game)` publishes the shared `impact` record (impact longitude,
  touchdown velocities, coast time, step count, validity, the wrap-aware signed
  error from the selected pad, and the two arrival flags); `markerX` returns the
  viewport's periodic copy. Scalars only: nothing is allocated per frame, and the
  live rocket state is read, never written.
- **`js/autopilot.js`** loses `updateLandingPrediction` and `landing` (the
  vacuum ballistic projection). **`js/main.js`** predicts once per frame after
  the physics budget. **`index.html`** loads the module between physics and
  autopilot; **`js/namespaces.js`** registers `R.trajectory` and `R.aerodynamics`
  (which previously only self-registered).
- **`js/render.js`.** The ground marker is drawn for manual flight too, from the
  periodic longitude — which always projects inside the viewport, so the marker
  can no longer disappear on a long coast, and at the seam it is drawn twice
  like the pads are — labelled `COAST` above the pad-name band, and coloured by
  the predicted arrival (teal = delivery on the target pad, amber = safe landing
  elsewhere, red = outside the world's limits). Two HUD rows: `COAST IMPACT` — the signed
  distance from the selected pad from a preallocated unit table (` m W`, ` km E`,
  …), or `ON TARGET PAD`, or `OUT OF HORIZON` — and `COAST TOUCHDOWN` with the
  predicted vertical / horizontal rates, coloured by the same test. The panel
  grew 345 → 387 px and the time-scale row and the key help moved down; no
  per-frame string concatenation.
- **`js/mission.js`.** The report gains the measured `touchdownVerticalSpeed`,
  `touchdownHorizontalSpeed` and the signed `targetError`, and the debrief quotes
  them next to the 0.3.1/0.3.2 envelope figures: `Touchdown 3.8 m/s down and
  0.4 m/s across, 12 m from Eastport centre.`, `1.2 km west of Farport`,
  `5.0 km east of Eastport`. Measured, not predicted: `physics.step` interpolates
  the ground crossing, so those velocities are the ones the rocket arrived with.
- **Carried over from the 0.3.2 review.** The shipped marker projected a raw
  longitude, so a coast landing more than two thirds of a circumference from the
  camera's left edge drew nothing even though that ground was on screen; the
  periodic copy fixes it, and the smoke test now asserts the property instead of
  leaving it to luck. `js/namespaces.js` registers `R.aerodynamics`, which until
  now only self-registered. The narrow-viewport branch of the flight panel clamps
  to the canvas, which the taller panel made worth doing.
- **`experiments/envelope.js`** gains per-route `touchdown V/H` and `pad error`
  columns and a worst-touchdown summary, so 0.5 tunes hard-landing stress on
  measured arrivals.
- **Experiments.** `experiments/trajectory-test.js` (new suite) and
  `experiments/impact-accuracy.js` (accuracy and cost, prediction step
  selectable) with logs in `experiments/logs/0.3.3-*.txt`.

## Measurements
Coast-forecast error against the same integrator at 24x the step
(`0.3.3-impact-accuracy.txt`; tolerance `max(25 m, 1% of range)`). 1,384 states
were forecast — 240 synthetic per world plus 424 sampled from every directed
reference route under both profiles — and 1,176 of them reached the ground in
both the forecast and the reference:

| Step | Worst range error | Over tolerance | Cost per forecast |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 1/120 s (shipped) | 37.8 m (0.45% of range) | 0 | 86–187 µs flown, ≤ 501 µs edge grid |
| 1/30 s (rejected) | 150.6 m (1.84%) | 8 (Cinder 4, Gossamer 4) | 23–47 µs flown, ≤ 133 µs edge grid |

Per world at the shipped step: Verdant 1.7 / 2.2 m, Tinmoon 2.3 / 1.7 m,
Cinder 2.1 / 37.8 m, Gossamer 1.6 / 12.4 m (flown routes / edge grid). 208 of
the 960 edge states coast past the 120 s horizon — high-altitude states on
worlds whose radius is only 2.5–4.1 km, where gravity at 9 km has fallen to
5–10% of surface gravity — and are reported invalid rather than stale.

Touchdown baseline for 0.5 (`0.3.3-touchdowns.txt`, reference build, all 12
directed routes, 12/12 delivered on every world and profile):

| World | Balanced worst touchdown | Gentle | Limits off |
|---|---|---|---|
| Verdant | 3.7 m/s down, 1 m off the pad | 3.5 m/s, 1 m | 3.7 m/s, 1 m |
| Tinmoon | 3.8 m/s down, 0 m | 3.7 m/s, 0 m | 3.8 m/s, 0 m |
| Cinder | 3.3 m/s down, 1 m | 2.7 m/s, 1 m | 3.3 m/s, 1 m |
| Gossamer | 3.8 m/s down, 0 m | 3.7 m/s, 0 m | 3.8 m/s, 0 m |

The envelope limits and the profiles barely move the arrival: every reference
route lands under 4 m/s down and within 1 m of the pad centre, against landing
limits of 8–15 m/s vertical and 18–30 m/s horizontal. That band is the
zero-stress baseline 0.5 should key damage on.

## Observed, not changed
- The forecast is a *coast*: under the autopilot it usually points somewhere the
  rocket will not land, because the guidance keeps burning. The HUD wording
  (`COAST IMPACT`, `COAST TOUCHDOWN`) says so; a powered-trajectory predictor
  stays deferred until it can be validated the same way.
- `trajectory.update` runs every frame in `main.update` even when the flight is
  over (it returns immediately, `phase !== 'flying'`), and the cost is per
  coast duration, not per frame rate: a 4x time scale predicts as often as 1x.
- The 0.3.2 items stand: `flight.usedAutopilot` is fixed at launch (0.4 economy),
  Tinmoon's 14 m small rebound before touchdown predates this slice, and the
  hover-blind staging rule is now written up as proposed 0.3.4.

## Validation
- `node experiments/trajectory-test.js` — passed: a rollout at the live fixed
  step reproduces an engine-off `physics.step` flight to 1e-6 m / 1e-9 m/s /
  1e-9 s; a prediction leaves the state, its throttle, its stage fuel, the cash
  and the phase untouched; a vacuum coast keeps `vx` exactly, is independent of
  attitude, and conserves energy against the inverse-square field within 1%
  (`v² = v₀² + 2GM(1/r₁ − 1/r₀)`, not a constant-`g` parabola — Tinmoon's radius
  is 2.5 km, so that approximation is 15% off at 500 m); drag on Gossamer
  carries the rocket under 90% of the drag-free range, arrives slower and takes
  longer; zero airspeed lands exactly where it hovers; a ground state and a
  beyond-horizon state publish no forecast while a longer step budget does find
  the ground; wrap longitudes stay unbounded while the pad error is wrap-aware
  and the marker is the viewport's periodic copy of the same ground (checked with
  a long westward Tinmoon coast that falls off the left edge).
- Mutation-checked: no drag in the rollout, no drag in the rollout *and* in
  `physics.step`, no horizon cap, a rollout that writes the live state,
  `predictCoast` nudging the state, a pad error without `wrapDelta`, a marker
  without the periodic copy, a renderer that projects the raw impact longitude,
  and a renderer that drops the seam copies each fail a named assertion.
- `node experiments/flight-test.js` — passed, with new assertions for the
  measured touchdown figures and the three debrief phrasings (on the pad centre,
  east of it, west of it).
- `node experiments/render-smoke-test.js` — passed, with new assertions that the
  coast rows are drawn in manual flight, that a real forecast replaces the
  horizon notice and labels the marker, that an invalid forecast draws the notice
  and no marker, and that the marker comes from the periodic copy of the impact
  longitude (a coast a whole world east is drawn once inside the viewport, and at
  the seam it is drawn on both edges).
- `node experiments/coords-test.js`, `aerodynamics-test.js`, `builder-test.js`,
  `autopilot-test.js` — passed, unchanged.
- `node experiments/envelope.js <world> 0 <off|balanced|gentle>` — 12/12
  delivered on all four worlds and every variant, as in 0.3.2: no guidance or
  physics behaviour changed in this slice.
- `node --check` for all source and experiment scripts; tabs, LF, no trailing
  whitespace.

## Next step
Implement 0.3.4: stage on authority rather than only on an empty tank — when the
active stage's `thrust / mass` cannot hold the guidance's hover multiple of local
gravity and a later living stage can, separate early — and judge it with
`experiments/build-sweep.js` on the same seeds, as flights fixed against flights
broken, while the reference routes keep their times, fuel and touchdown figures.
That is the main remaining source of autopilot failures (about 6% of random
builds with no limits, 5% Balanced, 4% Gentle). After that, 0.4 (economy, parts,
market) can price flights against the measured baseline in `0.4-plan.md`, which
now includes measured arrivals.

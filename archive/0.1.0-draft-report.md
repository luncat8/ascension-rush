### Plan layout
| File | Subject | Key deliverables |
|---|---|---|
| `0.1-plan.md` | World, rendering, planet & pads | Canvas scaffold, RAF loop, wrap-X planet in bottom 10 % strip, **log-Y scale** design, launch pads, triangle rocket sprite, camera with world↔screen transforms. No physics yet. |
| `0.2-plan.md` | Physics, stage builder, flight controls | Staged rocket model (mass/thrust/Isp/Tsiolkovsky), fixed-substep integrator, drag + inverse-square gravity, mouse heading, Space=stage, Shift/Ctrl=throttle, builder UI (stages/fuel/strength/payload), pad landing judgement, first-cut economy ($/kg fuel/steel/delivery), in-flight HUD. |
| `0.3-plan.md` | Autopilot & planet presets | Planet preset table (Verdant / Tinmoon / Cinder / Gossamer) with g0, atmo, size, prices; generalized physics reads from `game.planet`; state-machine autopilot (vertical climb → gravity turn → coast → entry brake → terminal descent → touchdown) using the same commands a player uses, with per-planet PID tuning and a headless tuning experiment; autopilot fee, projected landing marker. |
| `0.4-plan.md` | Economy balance, market, parts | Revised cost model (reward − fuel − new parts − repair − service − AP fee); proper parts catalog (engines, tanks, fairings) with mass, cost, service life, overhaul; per-pad drifting prices and contract board with urgency/fragility; `experiments/balance.js` Monte Carlo to tune profit margins (~10–20 % for well-built rockets, negative for overbuilt/crashed). |
| `0.5-plan.md` | Damage/failures & competitor AI | Stress model (G-stress, max-Q, burn wear, hard landing, separation shock); failure modes (engine partial/total out, tank leak, fairing loss, payload damage, RUD); repair cost, part wear across flights; rival company that bids on contracts, builds rockets and flies them via the same autopilot (headless sims), affects market prices, and is beatable; failure-rate Monte Carlo tuning. |
